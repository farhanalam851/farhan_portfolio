Privacy policy

Last updated: {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }}

This website is the personal portfolio of {{ p.name }} ({{ p.location }}). I keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the site and reply to the people who contact me. This page explains what that is.

1. Who is responsible

{{ p.name }} is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach me at {{ p.email }}.

2. What I collect and why

Contact form

If you send a message I receive your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. I use them only to reply to you and to discuss work or an opportunity. The message is stored in the site's database and a copy is emailed to me.

I keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them, and normally delete them within 12 months unless we start working together or you ask me to keep in touch.

Analytics (only if you accept)

If you click Accept analytics in the cookie banner, I load Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from (country or city level), and what device and browser they use. This helps me improve the site. It does not identify you by name. Google may process this data on servers outside India. If you click Decline, the analytics script is never loaded.

Server logs

Like every website, the hosting provider's servers record technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type and time, for security and reliability. I do not use these logs to track individuals.

4. Third-party services

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, and Bootstrap, icons and technology logos from the jsDelivr content network. Those providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request, as with any web resource. They are not used for advertising by me. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram take you to services with their own privacy policies.

5. What I do not do

  • I do not sell or rent your data.
  • I do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • I do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR in Europe), you can ask me to show you the data I hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent for analytics at any time. Email {{ p.email }} and I will respond promptly.

7. Children

This site is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

If I change how the site handles data, I will update this page and the date at the top.

9. Questions or complaints

Write to {{ p.email }}. If you are not satisfied, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country.
